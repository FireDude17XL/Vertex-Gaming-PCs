Common questions

Can I change a preconfigured system?

Yes. Choose Configure System, then change any component in the PC Builder.

Are frame rates guaranteed?

No. The figures are illustrative estimates based on the supplied reference, not verified benchmarks. Game settings, drivers, and hardware affect results.

Can I place an order here?

This site prepares and confirms builds locally. It does not take payments or transmit orders.

What should I include in a repair request?

Include your system specs, the issue, any error messages, and what you have already tried. Never include passwords.
