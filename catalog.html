Where the models come from

The CPU, GPU, and RAM lists are a curated import from Doc Oliver’s PC Part Dataset, an MIT-licensed collection of PCPartPicker catalog data. The source snapshot is dated July 23, 2025. View the imported data or read its license.

Pricing and availability

CPU, graphics, and memory prices are historical snapshot prices rounded to US dollars. Other component prices are Vertex planning allowances. This is not a live retailer inventory: no item is represented as in stock, and no price is a current offer. Final model numbers, prices, and availability are confirmed before an order.

Performance and compatibility

FPS ranges are illustrative planning estimates, not measured benchmarks. Hardware scores and clearance rules are maintained locally. Minecraft RTX estimates are omitted for non-GeForce selections because this model does not provide a comparable estimate. Final assembly requires checking manufacturer specifications, BIOS support, memory QVL, power connectors, and physical fit.

About the 3D preview

Locally generated schematic models respond to your choices. The preview shows layout and style, not exact retail-model geometry, cable routing, or a certified fit. It runs locally with Three.js and does not upload your configuration.
