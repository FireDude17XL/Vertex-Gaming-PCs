1-year limited warranty.

Straightforward coverage for every new Core, Pro, Ultra, and MAX system.

Vertex Gaming · Limited warranty · Effective September 26, 2026

What’s covered

Vertex Gaming covers defects in the parts it supplies and in its assembly workmanship for one year from the date your new system is delivered or collected. Coverage is included at no additional charge for the original purchaser of a new Vertex system.

How we make it right

For a covered defect, Vertex will diagnose and repair the system or replace the faulty part with a new or equivalent-function replacement at no charge for covered parts or labor. If a repair or equivalent replacement is not available within a reasonable time, Vertex will offer an appropriate refund for the affected product. Replacement parts remain covered for the balance of the original warranty or 90 days from repair, whichever is longer.

What’s excluded

This limited warranty does not cover accidental or liquid damage, misuse, improper power, cosmetic wear that does not affect operation, or damage caused by an incompatible upgrade, improper repair, or operation outside component specifications. Software problems, malware, data recovery, and parts not supplied by Vertex are not covered. Opening the case, upgrading a component, or using independent service does not by itself void this warranty; only damage caused by those actions is excluded.

Requesting service

  1. Contact Vertex using the contact details on your invoice. Provide the order number, delivery date, system serial number, symptoms, and any error messages.
  2. Vertex will help troubleshoot and, when necessary, arrange a return authorization and service instructions. Do not ship a system before receiving those instructions.
  3. Back up important files and remove personal accessories before service. Use protective packaging. Service may require reinstalling software; data backup and recovery are not included.

You pay shipping to Vertex; Vertex pays standard return shipping for a confirmed covered repair to the original delivery country. Any other shipping or non-covered service cost will be quoted for your approval before work begins. Local handoff may be arranged by agreement.

Manufacturer warranties & your rights

Some components may have separate manufacturer coverage beyond one year. That coverage is subject to the manufacturer’s terms and does not extend Vertex’s labor coverage. This warranty gives you specific legal rights, and you may also have other rights that vary by state or jurisdiction. It does not exclude or limit rights that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.

Prepare a warranty request →

The website prepares a downloadable request; it does not send it. Share it through the contact listed on your invoice. A copy of these terms can be requested with your quote or invoice.
